FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it work on QBCore and Qbox?

Yes. GF Police and GF Radio detect ESX Legacy, QBCore or Qbox automatically. They work with ox_inventory or qb-inventory, and with other inventories through the framework.

Which languages?

English and Spanish. Choose it in server.cfg with setr ox:locale en or es. Texts live in open files, so you can add your own language.

Can I edit the script?

Yes. Config, texts, the compatibility code for each framework, inventory and bank, and the notification style are open. Only the core is protected, with the official Cfx.re escrow system.

Do I pay for updates?

No. When a new version is out, download it again from portal.cfx.re with the same account.

Which servers can run it?

Servers whose license key belongs to the same Cfx.re account you bought with.

Do I need ox_doorlock for the jail?

It is not required, but we recommend it: the officer puts the suspect in a cell, locks the door with ox_doorlock and applies the sentence from the report.

Can I get a refund?

See our terms and refunds. If something does not work, message us on Discord first: it can almost always be fixed.

How do I get support?

On our Discord, in the customer support channel. Have your Tebex transaction ID ready and anything shown in red in the console.
